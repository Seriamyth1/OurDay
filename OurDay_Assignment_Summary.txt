OURDAY - TEAM CHARTER & PRODUCT VISION ASSIGNMENT SUMMARY
=========================================================

WHAT WE NEED TO MAKE
--------------------
All pages go in our team's Confluence space. For each page: create a new page,
open the "..." menu, choose "Templates and import", and search for the
template name.

1. DESIGN THE BOX  (template: "CSCI - Design the Box")  - 15 pts
   Think of it as the box our product would be sold in. It needs:
   - The product name (OurDay)
   - A short list of the main features (the front of the box)
   - A more detailed description of each feature (the back of the box)
   - The product requirements (system and user requirements)
   - A logo, either described in words or linked

2. PRODUCT VISION  (template: "CSCI - Product Vision")  - 10 pts
   One short statement in the standard format:
   FOR [target customer] WHO [need or problem], OurDay IS A [product category]
   THAT [key benefit]. UNLIKE [competitors], OUR PRODUCT [what makes it different].

3. TEAM CHARTER  (template: "CSCI - Team Charter")  - 15 pts
   How the team works together: member names and contact info, how we
   communicate, meeting times, what happens when someone misses a meeting,
   roles, and how we make decisions and settle disagreements.
   Fill in every field the template has.

4. REFERENCE PAGE
   A plain Confluence page with links to the three pages above.

When all four pages exist, submit the word "Completed" on the assignment.

GRADING NOTE: Each page gets full points only if every field in its template
is filled in and clear. A missing field drops it to 5 points.


WHAT WE ALREADY HAVE
--------------------
From "Presentation 1-2.pdf" (Ramir Moses, Heriberto Chavez-Pullman) - covers
most of the Design the Box content:

Name:
  - OurDay, a customizable calendar app

Features:
  - Easier to manage than other calendar apps
  - Daily, weekly and monthly refreshers plus reminders
  - More freedom in how you organize your calendar
  - Customizable backgrounds and themes

Requirements:
  - Works offline (Wi-Fi optional)
  - Runs on phone, tablet or computer
  - Under about 300 MB
  - Works on Windows, macOS, Linux and iOS
  - Notifications turned on

Tech:
  - Java, local storage, a UI framework, GitHub for version control

Code:
  - The "CalBackground" file is a Java class for customizable backgrounds
    (solid color, gradient or image). This shows the theme feature is already
    started - worth mentioning on the Design the Box page.

(The other PDF, "presentation.pdf", is the Git intro class activity and is not
related to this assignment.)
